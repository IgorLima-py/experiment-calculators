What this assumes, and what it does not cover
  • A detectable effect is not a visibility cliff. This is the most common way to misread the number. At 80% power, an effect exactly equal to the figure above has an 80% chance of reaching significance — not a certainty. Smaller effects are not invisible either; they are just detected less than 80% of the time. The number is a reliability threshold, not a threshold of existence.
  • Normal approximation to the binomial, pooled variance under the null, no continuity correction — the same conventions as the sample size calculator, since this is its exact inverse.
  • An even 50/50 split and steady traffic for the whole window.
  • One primary metric, analysed once at the end of the window.
  • Not covered: revenue and other continuous metrics, more than two variants, sequential designs, and any allowance for traffic that varies by day of week.
